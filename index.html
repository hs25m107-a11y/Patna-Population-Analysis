Population Analysis of Patna

This study examines the spatial distribution and temporal change of residential population in Patna from 2000 to 2020 using the GHSL Population dataset.

5. Overall Change

Between 2000 and 2020, the population of Patna increased from 1,748,350 to 2,507,450, an increase of 759,100 people (43.42%).

6. Discussion

The maps show the spatial distribution of residential population across Patna. Population increased between 2000 and 2020, with higher population concentrations visible in built-up parts of the city. The temporal maps help identify how the population distribution changed over the study period.

The analysis uses GHSL gridded population data at 100 m resolution. Therefore, the results represent estimated population distribution rather than individual household-level population. Differences in boundary definitions and population estimation methods may also affect the results.
